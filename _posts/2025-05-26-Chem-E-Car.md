The vehicle, detailed in Sharkus_Electrus_Poster.jpg, utilizes a propulsion mechanism powered by Thermoelectric Generators (TEGs) that convert a temperature gradient into electrical current via the Seebeck effect. A large temperature difference is generated by pairing an extreme cold sink with an intense heat source. The hot side is fueled by an exothermic acid-base reaction mixing aqueous hydrochloric acid and solid sodium hydroxide, utilizing pumps to ensure thorough mixing. The cold sink consists of dry ice (CO₂) and aluminum heat sinks. Structurally, eight TEGs are housed within a 3D-printed carbon fiber container, configured with the dry ice at the bottom, the TEGs in the middle, and the heated reaction on top to deliver a high, stabilizing initial voltage.

The stopping mechanism is driven by a Chemical Chameleon reaction. By tracking the reduction of potassium permanganate, an Arduino Uno paired with a TCS 3200 Color Sensor monitors the color change to halt the vehicle precisely at the calculated time.
